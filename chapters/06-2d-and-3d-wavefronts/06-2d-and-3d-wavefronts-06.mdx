## 6.6 Chapter summary (ESBNQ)

[Presentation:
        23VM](http://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/embed_code/19768592)

- A wavefront is an imaginary line that connects waves that are in phase.
- Huygens' Principle states that every point of a wave front serves as a point source of spherical, secondary
            waves. After a time t, the new position of the wave front will be that of a surface tangent to the secondary
            waves.
- Diffraction is the ability of a wave to spread out in wavefronts as the wave passes through a small aperture
            or around a sharp edge.
- When a wave passes through a slit, diffraction of the wave occurs. Diffraction of the wave is when the
            wavefront spreads out or “bends” around corners.
- The degree of diffraction depends on the width of the slit and the wavelength of the wave with:
            \(\text{diffraction}\propto \frac{\lambda}{w}\) where \(\lambda\) is the wavelength of the wave and \(w\) is
            the width of the slit.
