### Textbook Exercise 10.3

**Problem:**
What did Hans Oersted discover about the relationship between electricity and
                                                magnetism?

**Solution:**
He discovered that electricity and magnetism were related to one another. By
                                                passing an electric current through a metal wire suspended above a
                                                magnetic compass, Oersted was able to produce a definite motion of the
                                                compass needle in response to the current.

**Problem:**
A uniform magnetic field of \(\text{0,35}\) \(\text{T}\) in the
                                                        vertical direction exists. A piece of cardboard, of surface area
                                                        \(\text{0,35}\) \(\text{m$^{2}$}\) is placed flat on a
                                                        horizontal surface inside the field. What is the magnetic flux
                                                        through the cardboard?

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
                                                \phi & = BA \cos \theta \\
                                                & = (\text{0,35})(\text{0,35}) \\
                                                & = \text{0,1225}\text{ Wb}
                                                \end{align*}

**Problem:**
The one edge is then lifted so that the cardboard is now inclined at
                                                        \(\text{17}\)\(\text{°}\) to the positive \(x\)-direction.
                                                        What is the magnetic flux through the cardboard? What will the
                                                        induced emf be if the field drops to zero in the space of
                                                        \(\text{3}\) \(\text{s}\)? Why?

**Solution:**
When the piece of cardboard is inclined the flux is:

                                                \begin{align*}
                                                \phi & = BA \cos \theta \\
                                                & = (\text{0,35})(\text{0,35}) \cos (\text{17}) \\
                                                & = \text{0,117}
                                                \end{align*}



The change in flux is \(\text{0,117}\) \(\text{Wb}\) and the induced
                                                        emf will be zero because the cardboard is not a conductor.

**Problem:**
Consider a square coil of 3 turns with a side length of \(\text{1,56}\)
                                                \(\text{m}\). The coil is subjected to a varying magnetic field that
                                                changes uniformly from \(\text{4,38}\) \(\text{T}\) to \(\text{0,35}\)
                                                \(\text{T}\) in an interval of \(\text{3}\) \(\text{minutes}\). The axis
                                                of the solenoid makes an angle of \(\text{197}\)\(\text{°}\) to the
                                                magnetic field. Find the induced emf.

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
                                        \mathcal{E} &= N\frac{\Delta\phi}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N\frac{\phi_{f} - \phi_{i}}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N\frac{B_{f}A\cos \theta - B_{i}A \cos \theta}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N\frac{A\cos \theta(B_{f} - B_{i})}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = 3 \left( \frac{(\text{1,56})^{2} \cos (\text{197})(\text{0,35} -
                                        \text{4,38})}{180} \right) \\
                                        & = \text{0,16}\text{ V}
                                        \end{align*}

Consider a solenoid coil of 13 turns with radius \(\text{6,8} \times
                                                \text{10}^{-\text{2}}\) \(\text{m}\). The solenoid is subjected to a

                                                \(\text{T}\) to \(\text{1,8}\) \(\text{T}\) in an interval of
                                                \(\text{18}\) \(\text{s}\). The axis of the solenoid makes an angle of
                                                \(\text{88}\)\(\text{°}\) to the magnetic field.

**Problem:**
Find the induced emf.

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
                                                \mathcal{E} &= N\frac{\Delta\phi}{\Delta t} \\
                                                & = N\frac{\phi_{f} - \phi_{i}}{\Delta t} \\
                                                & = N\frac{B_{f}A\cos \theta - B_{i}A \cos \theta}{\Delta t} \\
                                                & = N\frac{A\cos \theta(B_{f} - B_{i})}{\Delta t} \\

                                                \cos (\text{88})(\text{1,8} - (-\text{5}))}{18} \right) \\
                                                & = \text{0,00249}\text{ V}
                                                \end{align*}

**Problem:**

Find the change in flux if the emf is \(\text{0,12}\) \(\text{V}\) over a

**Problem:**
Consider a rectangular coil of area \(\text{1,73}\) \(\text{m$^{2}$}\). The
                                                coil is subjected to a varying magnetic field that changes uniformly
                                                from \(\text{2}\) \(\text{T}\) to \(\text{10}\) \(\text{T}\) in an
                                                interval of \(\text{3}\) \(\text{ms}\). The axis of the solenoid makes
                                                an angle of \(\text{55}\)\(\text{°}\) to the magnetic field. Find
                                                the induced emf.

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
                                        \mathcal{E} &= N\frac{\Delta\phi}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N\frac{\phi_{f} - \phi_{i}}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N\frac{B_{f}A\cos \theta - B_{i}A \cos \theta}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N\frac{A\cos \theta(B_{f} - B_{i})}{\Delta t} \\
                                        & = N \left( \frac{(\text{1,73}) \cos (\text{55})(\text{10} - \text{2})}{3}
                                        \right) \\
                                        & = \text{2,65}\text{ V}
                                        \end{align*}
